Operating principles

How I think about defense.

Six principles that decide where automation, controls, and attention go.

Defense in Depth

No single control is trusted to hold. Layered EDR, network, identity, and email controls mean a bypass at one layer meets resistance at the next.

Automation First

Every recurring investigation becomes a workflow. SOAR, FalconPy, and PowerShell turn one-off triage into repeatable, auditable response.

Least Privilege

Access is scoped to the task, identities are continuously validated, and standing privilege is treated as a liability to be eliminated.

Continuous Validation

Posture decays. Coverage gaps, stale agents, and drifting policies are hunted continuously — not rediscovered during an incident.

Visibility Before Control

You can't defend what you can't see. Asset inventory and telemetry coverage come first; enforcement comes once the blind spots are mapped.

Risk-Based Prioritization

Not every CVE matters equally. Remediation is sequenced by exploitability, exposure, and business impact — not raw CVSS counts.
